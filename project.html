Background

Problem & Motivation

The Problem

Independent restaurant managers regularly hear positive feedback from guests in person, but that feedback rarely makes it onto public review platforms. Guests may intend to leave a review but forget, find the process inconvenient, or not know what to write. Meanwhile, managers at independent restaurants often lack the marketing resources larger chains have to actively solicit online reviews. The result is that satisfied guest experiences go unrepresented online.

The Concept

ReviewCraft is an in-progress group project concept designed to address this challenge. The idea is to help restaurant managers capture feedback they have heard from a guest and generate a review draft from it. The guest then reviews the draft and chooses whether to post it. This is a concept currently in development as a group project, not a finished, tested, or deployed application.

Contributions

My Contributions So Far

As part of this in-progress group project, my contributions so far include helping define the problem and target users, and working on the product vision, personas, and epics.

Helping Define the Problem

Helped articulate the central issue: positive in-person feedback given directly to managers is routinely lost because guests rarely follow through with posting online reviews afterwards.

Helping Define Target Users

Collaborated on identifying the primary audience: independent restaurant managers who interact directly with diners but lack dedicated tools to translate verbal compliments into public reviews.

Working on Product Vision

Worked on shaping the product vision: a manager inputs spoken feedback, a review draft is generated, and the guest retains complete authority to review, edit, and choose whether to post it.

Working on Personas

Helped develop user personas capturing the key participants: the busy restaurant manager seeking authentic reviews and the guest who needs an effortless way to share their experience.

Working on Epics

Contributed to drafting high-level epics that structure the concept's workflow, including feedback capture, draft generation, and guest review and posting approval.

Context

Current Status & Limitations

ReviewCraft is an in-progress group project concept. The app is not finished, tested, or deployed. No software has been built, no technology stack or framework has been finalized, and no user testing or quantitative results exist at this stage.
